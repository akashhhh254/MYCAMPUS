## 3. Strict Role-Based Architecture (3 Roles ONLY)

The MyCampus platform strictly defines **three roles**:

1. **`PRINCIPAL`** (Admin & Executive Control)
2. **`TEACHER`** (Faculty & Academic Control)
3. **`STUDENT`** (Learner & Academic Consumer)

*(All references to previous "Dean" roles or demo accounts have been completely purged from the codebase).*

---

## 4. Student Authentication (College ID ONLY)

Students log in exclusively with their **College ID** and password:
- No Google Sign-In button on Student Login screen.
- No personal email login.
- No phone number / OTP login.
- **Forgot Password:** Students enter their **College ID**. The backend matches their student record and sends the reset link to their official recovery email on file.

## 5. Staff Authentication (Teacher & Principal)

- **Teacher Portal:** Supports Teacher ID / Official Email login or 1-tap **Google Workspace Sign-In**. Validates role == `teacher`.
- **Principal Portal:** Supports Principal ID / Official Email login or 1-tap **Google Workspace Sign-In**. Validates role == `principal`.
- If an unauthorized user or student attempts to log in through staff portals, access is rejected immediately on the server/auth layer.
